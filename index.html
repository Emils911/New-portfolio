PeerPath

PeerPath is a nationwide digital campus network that breaks down institutional silos to connect university students across the country on a single, secure platform. Verified exclusively through `.edu` emails, it provides a unified ecosystem where students can find cross-campus project partners, share academic resources, and build organic communities. By bridging the gap between campuses, PeerPath transforms a fragmented university landscape into a collaborative, nationwide student network.

Campus Cart

CampusCart is a secure, student-exclusive marketplace designed to simplify how university students buy, sell, and exchange items within their campus community. Verified entirely through .edu email addresses, the platform eliminates the safety concerns and logistical headaches of public classified sites, creating a trusted local network. Whether students are looking to unload expensive textbooks at the end of a semester, find affordable dorm furniture, or safely sublet an apartment, CampusCart directly connects peers on campus for seamless, face-to-face transactions.
